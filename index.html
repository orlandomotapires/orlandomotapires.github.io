Thesis and software

2026

Hybrid Quantum-Classical Graph Partitioning Algorithm for Domain Decomposition

Bachelor’s thesis, Universidade Senai Cimatec. Supervised by MSc. Stefan Schröder and Dr. Anderson Buarque.

A method and implementation for partitioning simulation domains with a hybrid quantum–classical algorithm, applied to manufacturing simulations at Fraunhofer IPT.

  • Thesis (PDF, 4.6 MB)
  • The software: HDD Studio
2025 – 2026

HDD Studio

Hybrid domain decomposition, the software behind my thesis

A desktop app and command-line tool that reorders any square sparse matrix into diagonal blocks, by partitioning its graph with METIS, quantum annealing or QAOA, locally or on D-Wave and QLM quantum hardware.

  • Code on GitHub
2026

Ket Lab

A quantum computing course that runs in the browser

Write Qiskit or PennyLane code and watch the quantum state change line by line. 53 lessons in 7 tracks take you from a single qubit to quantum annealing, with nothing to install.
